# Changelog

All the updates to Ages at War, newest first.

## October 31, 2026

### Battlefields
- Backgrounds come alive: clouds drift, waves sparkle and wash up the beach, the valley river flows, and palms, trees and pines sway in the wind.
- Birds fly over most maps, vultures circle the canyon, and tumbleweeds roll across it.
- The jungle sun no longer has a green haze.
- The battlefield preview in battle setup looks the same on phones as on PC, instead of zoomed in.

### Troops and combat
- In the Stone Age each fortress keeps just its two starting wall defenders; more can be added from the Bronze Age, so the opening has to be fought in the field.
- The computer goes for zones: when the armies are about even it sends its troops to the nearest zone it doesn't hold, as long as the enemy near it is weaker, and keeps the furthest zone it does hold otherwise. Well ahead, it attacks and takes zones on the way.
- Fortresses always mend slowly, even under attack and in the Future age, and faster once the walls have been quiet. The 25-minute overtime that crumbled the front fortresses is gone.
- Wall defenders' shots leave from their weapons (barrel, bow or throwing hand) instead of from the middle of their bodies.
- Every wall defender now shows on the walls and takes a turn shooting (up to 10 with Garrison), instead of at most seven. They stand closer together once there are more than seven.
- The computer sizes up the enemy army before attacking. Up against a bigger army (for example one gathered on a zone), it holds its troops on the zone nearest its walls and attacks together once it has caught up, instead of sending troops in one at a time. The Easy computer still charges in.
- Troops ordered to hold a zone now fall back to it even mid-fight, striking at anything in reach as they go. Shields and medics obey too.
- Catapults and other siege weapons lob their shots at where the target stood and land there, splashing everyone in the blast, instead of following a moving troop.
- Every heavy troop is melee: the Bronze Age War Elephant's rider now carries a spear instead of a bow.
- Spearmen carry their round shield on the far (left) arm, seen side-on, instead of over their chest.
- Medics set to Front or Back now heal on both sides.
- Medics can stay with the front or the back line, and medics set to Shields follow them and heal them.
- Wall defenders take turns evenly and rest when there's nothing to shoot.

### Upgrades and controls
- On wide screens the upgrades wrap around your controls: Troops fill the space to the left, Economy sits just above the troop buttons, and Fortress fills the space to the right. Narrower screens keep the bar above the controls.
- In the Future age, an upgrade at its level cap becomes a Boost: each one gives all your troops +1% health and damage, including troops already in the field. Boosts cost a little more each time; the computer buys them too once everything is maxed.
- Bigger text for the upgrades bar's header and the line describing each upgrade.
- The upgrades bar is split into three labeled groups: Troops on the left, Economy in the middle and Fortress on the right. On narrower screens the groups wrap onto a second line instead of sliding off the edge.
- Upgrade text is larger and easier to read; on wide screens each card shows icon, name and price on one line.
- A new gold research bar sits beside the training bar, so you can follow both at once.
- Upgrade prices depend on the upgrade's level, not your age.
- Troop buttons show each troop's class, and tooltips name it.

### Sound
- Animal calls (horse neighs, camel grunts, wolf snarls and barks, mammoth and elephant trumpets) and creaking timber on catapults, siege engines and wrecks no longer sound electronic: they use natural rendered voices and real stick-slip wood creaks.
- Troop voices sound human instead of robotic: grunts, effort sounds and a shout as troops march out.
- Each melee weapon has its own sound: swords swing and clang or cut, spears and pikes thrust, clubs swing and land, shields bash.
- More realistic effects: layered gunfire, ringing steel, plucked bows, real explosions and engines.

### Graphics
- The Stone Age cave is one solid rocky hill with the log deck resting on top.
- World War and Modern troops wear trousers up to the belt, and Future troops have an armoured waist with a ball joint at the hips, so no torso floats above its legs.
- All six fortresses redrawn: sunlit masonry with mortar, chips and moss, proper gates (stone arches, studded doors, a portcullis, a riveted blast door), torches and lit windows, plus period details: a palisade and cave fire, a painted frieze and stepped battlements, a cone-roofed keep with ivy, a brick bastion with cannon and a sentry box, a bunker with camo netting and barbed wire, and a glowing future citadel.
- Better troop art: faces, hands, a proper stride, shading, and more detailed mounts and machines.

### Online and lobby
- The lobby keeps joined players when the lineup changes, and allows at most two players per side on one phone.
- Zones and orders pass to a teammate when a fortress falls.
- Kill bounties go only to commanders who are still standing.
- Gas clouds no longer flicker.

## October 30, 2026

### New features
- Three capture zones across the field. Hold one with only your troops to take it; each zone adds 10% to your income.
- Orders: send your troops to attack or to hold zone 1, 2 or 3.
- Healer targeting: Default (most hurt), Front, Back, or a troop type to look after first.
- Study (gold becomes experience) and War chest (experience becomes gold), once per age each.
- Each age has its own barrage: arrows, shells, gas, drones and more.
- Tooltips name each age's troops.
- Shield bearers who march to the front and soak up hits.
- The mouse wheel pans the battlefield.
- Tilt to scroll: tip your phone to slide the battlefield (works on Chrome and iPhone).

### Upgrades panel
- A compact upgrades bar above the troop buttons, or a Large panel that spells out each upgrade (set in Options).
- On phones the bar slides sideways, and upgrades are bought with a tap so you can swipe the list.
- Panels never overlap when players share a screen.
- All settings are in the pause menu.

### Civilizations
- Each civilization has its own look: troop gear, badges, pennants and fortress additions.

### Balance (from 19,000 simulated battles)
- Merchants +3% income, Sages -3% income.
- Warlords' troops are 7% less tough.
- Pikes have 135 health and cost 50 gold.

### Fixes
- Colors held by empty lineup slots can be picked again.
- Upgrade taps work with a second finger on the screen.
- Only the main mouse button buys.
- Day and night is set per device.

## October 29, 2026

### Launch
- Ages at War is playable in the browser, from the Stone Age to the Future.

### New features
- Pikes, healers, veteran ranks, tower upgrades and a once-per-battle emergency repair (tap twice to confirm).
- Nightmare and Insane computer opponents that attack in coordinated waves.
- Civilizations and a game speed option.
- Research slows with each level, and Scholars speed it up.
- A small chat for online games, with quick messages.
- A live preview of the chosen battlefield in battle setup.

### Polish
- Wheels roll forward, and shots leave from each weapon's barrel or throwing arm.
- Arrows and javelins look like the real thing.
- Quieter background sounds with their own volume option; waves only on water maps, plus tree and river ambience.

### Balance
- Siege and counters rebalanced.
- Healers heal up close, can't stack on one troop, and heal about a third as much as before.
