Options

Low graphics turns off weather and lighting effects and draws at a lower resolution, which helps older phones. On iPhone, if you hear nothing, check the ring/silent switch.

How to play

Tap a troop to pay for it and add it to your training line. Fighting earns experience; when the hourglass glows, tap it to enter the next age with stronger troops and a new fortress. Kills pay back 20% of a troop's cost. Drag the battlefield or tap the map at the top to look around.

The star button opens your upgrades. Each new age raises how far every upgrade can go and unlocks new ones, twenty-three in all. You can line up three at a time. With more commanders on one side, that side earns more gold in total, so 1 vs 2 or 1 vs 3 is a real underdog fight.

On a computer: 1–8 train troops, X cancel, Q defender, W barrage, E upgrades, R emergency repair, A advance age.
